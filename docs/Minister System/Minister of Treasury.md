# 🪙 Minister of Treasury
*   **Prefix:** `&2[MoT]`
*   **Focus:** Economy, taxes, and markets.
*   **Role:** Manages the server's economic stability and distributes wealth.

## Exclusive Perks
Each minister receives specific buffs tailored to their ministry role to assist with daily tasks.

*   **Role:** Money manager.
*   **Buffs:**
    *   **Luck II:** Increases the chance of obtaining rare items while fishing/looting chests.
    *   **Vault Bonus:** +15% more money from any source.
*   **Salary:** $2,500/day.

## Executive Orders
Each minister has access to a series of specific policies called **Cabinet Decisions**. These policies have a cost (from the National Treasury) and a specific duration.

| Decision | Duration | Effect | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TAX_HOLIDAY** | 24 Hours | Tax exemption. | No server transaction taxes. |
| **ECONOMIC_STIMULUS** | Instant | Direct Cash Assistance (BLT). | Distributes 10k Vault Points to all online players. |
| **AUCTION_BOOST** | 48 Hours | Auction market boost. | Doubles auction limits & reduces admin fees. |
| **TREASURY_BONUS** | 12 Hours | State dividends. | +50% money from mob kills & mining. |
| **MARKET_CRASH** | 6 Hours | Market crisis simulation. | 40% discount in the Admin Shop (Flash Sale). |
