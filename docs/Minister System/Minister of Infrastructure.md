# 🏗️ Minister of Infrastructure
*   **Prefix:** `&7[MoI]`
*   **Focus:** Construction, land claims, and materials.
*   **Role:** Facilitates town building and public projects.

## Exclusive Perks
Each minister receives specific buffs tailored to their ministry role to assist with daily tasks.

*   **Role:** Town builder.
*   **Buffs:**
    *   **Haste II:** Instant mining speed (Insta-mine for stone/deepslate).
    *   **Land Claims:** Bonus +50 Claim Blocks every day.
*   **Salary:** $2,500/day.

## Executive Orders
Each minister has access to a series of specific policies called **Cabinet Decisions**. These policies have a cost (from the National Treasury) and a specific duration.

| Decision | Duration | Effect | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CONSTRUCTION_BOOM** | 24 Hours | Construction acceleration. | Global Haste III + Jump Boost II effect. |
| **FREE_REAL_ESTATE** | 48 Hours | Land subsidy. | 50% discount on claim block costs. |
| **BUILDERS_PARADISE** | 12 Hours | Builder's paradise. | Double building material drops (stone, wood). |
| **PUBLIC_WORKS** | 30 Minutes | Public projects. | Building materials rain down at spawn. |
| **INFRASTRUCTURE_GRANT** | Instant | Infrastructure grant. | Grants 50k to 10 random players for building. |
