# 💰 Minister of Commerce
*   **Prefix:** `&9[MoC]`
*   **Focus:** Player-to-player trade, shops, and resources.
*   **Role:** Increases trading activity and the availability of rare items.

## Exclusive Perks
Each minister receives specific buffs tailored to their ministry role to assist with daily tasks.

*   **Role:** Money manager.
*   **Buffs:**
    *   **Luck II:** Increases the chance of obtaining rare items while fishing/looting chests.
    *   **Vault Bonus:** +15% more money from any source.
*   **Salary:** $2,500/day.

## Executive Orders
Each minister has access to a series of specific policies called **Cabinet Decisions**. These policies have a cost (from the National Treasury) and a specific duration.

| Decision | Duration | Effect | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TRADE_FAIR** | 24 Hours | Trade fair. | Spawns NPC traders with exclusive items. |
| **RESOURCE_BOOM** | 12 Hours | Resource explosion. | x2 drop rate for mining, farming, fishing. |
| **MERCHANT_CARAVAN** | 6 Hours | Merchant caravan. | NPC Caravan with rare items at low prices. |
| **BLACK_FRIDAY** | 48 Hours | Massive promotion. | Free shop advertising & increased visibility. |
| **TREASURE_HUNT** | Instant | Treasure hunt. | Scatters 20 treasure chests across the map. |
