# 🛡️ Minister of Defense
*   **Prefix:** `&4[MoD]`
*   **Focus:** Server security, PvP, and territory protection.
*   **Role:** Responsible for security stability and managing combat events.

## Exclusive Perks
Each minister receives specific buffs tailored to their ministry role to assist with daily tasks.

*   **Role:** Combat commander.
*   **Buffs:**
    *   **Strength I:** Increased attack damage.
    *   **Resistance I:** Reduced incoming damage.
    *   **Attributes:** +10% Damage, +8% Defense.
*   **Salary:** $2,500/day.

## Executive Orders

Each minister has access to a series of specific policies called **Cabinet Decisions**. These policies have a cost (from the National Treasury) and a specific duration.

| Decision | Duration | Effect | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DECLARE_WAR** | 24 Hours | Activates War Games Event. | Open PvP zone with double loot. |
| **MILITARY_DRAFT** | - | Mandatory military service & PvP tournament. | Grants basic combat items to all players. |
| **DEFENSE_PROTOCOL** | 12 Hours | National defense protocol. | Reduces damage taken by all players by 20%. |
| **ARMORY_DISCOUNT** | 24 Hours | Weapons subsidy. | 30% discount on weapons & armor at NPC shops. |
| **BORDER_PATROL** | 48 Hours | Border patrol. | Spawns elite mobs at borders with special drops. |
