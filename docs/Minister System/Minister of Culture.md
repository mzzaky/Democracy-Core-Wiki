# 🎨 Minister of Culture
*   **Prefix:** `&d[MoC]`
*   **Focus:** Events, entertainment, and community experience.
*   **Role:** Keeps community spirit high through festivals and celebrations.

## Exclusive Perks
Each minister receives specific buffs tailored to their ministry role to assist with daily tasks.

*   **Role:** Public icon.
*   **Buffs:**
    *   **Glowing:** Glowing effect (makes them easier to see during events).
    *   **Dolphin's Grace:** Faster swimming.
    *   **XP Bonus:** +10% Experience gain.
*   **Salary:** $2,500/day.

## Executive Orders
Each minister has access to a series of specific policies called **Cabinet Decisions**. These policies have a cost (from the National Treasury) and a specific duration.

| Decision | Duration | Effect | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FESTIVAL_WEEK** | 72 Hours | Festival week. | Minigame tournaments with rewards. |
| **DOUBLE_XP_WEEKEND** | 48 Hours | XP party. | 2x XP gain server-wide. |
| **FIREWORK_SHOW** | 24 Hours | Firework show. | Automatic firework shows every hour at spawn. |
| **CULTURAL_EXCHANGE** | 24 Hours | Cultural exchange. | NPC trading for rare decoration items. |
| **COMMUNITY_EVENT** | Instant | Community competition. | Starts a building/PvP event with a 500k reward. |
