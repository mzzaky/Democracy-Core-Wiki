# Overview

The Recall System is designed as a power-balancing mechanism (*checks and balances*). The process is intentionally challenging, requiring significant financial backing and substantial majority support to prevent minority groups from abusing the feature.

The recall process is divided into two main phases:
1.  **Petition Phase (`COLLECTING`):** Gathering support signatures.
2.  **Recall Vote Phase (`VOTING`):** A referendum to determine the president's fate.


## ⚙️ Important Configurations

Settings for this system can be found in `config.yml` under the `recall` section.

```yaml
recall:
  # Waiting time before a new petition can be created after a failure
  cooldown-days: 15
  
  # Duration for signature collection
  collection-days: 7
  
  # Duration of the recall voting phase
  voting-days: 3
  
  # Security deposit required for signers
  signature-deposit: 50000
  
  # Percentage of active players required to trigger a vote
  required-signature-percentage: 30
  
  # Percentage of 'REMOVE' votes required for success
  required-percentage: 60
```

## 🛡️ Security Mechanisms

To prevent *spam* and abuse:
1.  **High Deposits:** Prevents casual players from starting petitions without real support due to the high risk of losing money.
2.  **Active Player Calculation:** The required number of signatures is dynamic, calculated based on players who have logged in within the last 7 days, rather than the total registered player count (who might be inactive).
3.  **Super-Majority:** Requires 60% of votes to remove a president, ensuring the decision reflects the will of a significant majority, not just 50%+1.

## 🔧 Admin Commands

Admins have the authority to intervene in the recall process:
*   **Cancel Petition:** `/dc admin cancelrecall <reason>`
    *   Cancels the active petition and refunds all deposits (100% Refund).
*   **Forced Removal:** `/dc admin removepresident`
    *   Immediately removes the president without going through the voting process.
