🔄 Mechanism

1. Initiating a Petition​

Any player can start a recall petition against the currently serving president.

  • Requirements:
    • A president must be in office (cannot recall a former president).
    • The initiator cannot be the President themselves.
    • No other recall petition is currently active.
    • The global post-failure cooldown must have expired (Default: 15 Days).
    • The initiator must have a sufficient balance for the initial deposit.
  • Cost: The initiator must pay a deposit (Default: $50,000).
  • Command: /dc recall start

2. Signature Collection Phase (COLLECTING)​

Once started, the petition enters a signature collection period.

  • Duration: 7 Days (Default).
  • Activity: Other players can sign the petition to support the recall.
  • Signature Cost: Every signer must pay the same deposit as the initiator ($50,000).
    • This ensures that supporters are serious about the cause.
  • Target: The number of signatures must reach a certain percentage of the server's active population (Default: 30% of weekly active players).
  • Failure Consequences: If the target is not reached by the deadline, the petition fails, and all deposits are forfeited (transferred to the National Treasury).
  • Withdrawing Support: Players can withdraw their signatures but will only receive a 50% refund.

3. Referendum / Voting Phase (VOTING)​

If the signature target is met, the system automatically begins the voting phase.

  • Duration: 3 Days (Default).
  • Activity: All players on the server (including those who didn't sign the petition) can cast a vote.
  • Options:
    • REMOVE (Red): Agree to remove the President.
    • KEEP (Green): Agree to keep the President.
  • Command: /dc recall vote <remove/keep>

4. Results & Consequences​

✅ Success Scenario (President Impeached)​

Occurs if the REMOVE votes reach the super-majority threshold (Default: 60%).

  1. President Dismissed: The President immediately loses their position.
  2. Full Refund: All initiator and signer deposits are 100% refunded.
  3. Emergency Election: The system automatically triggers an Emergency Election.
  4. Sound Effect: A Wither Death sound is played globally.

❌ Failure Scenario (President Remains)​

Occurs if the REMOVE votes fall below the threshold (even if there are more REMOVE than KEEP votes, it can still fail if it doesn't reach 60%).

  1. President Secure: The term continues as normal.
  2. Forfeited Deposits: All initiator and signer deposits are seized and transferred to the National Treasury (FINE_INCOME).
  3. Cooldown: The recall system enters a cooldown period (Default: 15 Days) during which no new petitions can be created.
