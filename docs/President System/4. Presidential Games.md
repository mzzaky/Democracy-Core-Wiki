# ⚔️ Presidential Games

This document explains the technical aspects of **Presidential Arena Games**, a mass PvP event feature that can be organized by the President as a means of entertainment and wealth distribution for citizens.

## 📋 Overview

The Arena is a specialized battle zone (Free-For-All) where players can fight each other to compete for cash prizes, rare items, and glory. This event lasts for several days and features a daily leaderboard system.

## 🔧 Activation Mechanism

Only the President can start a new Arena session.
*   **Command:** `/dc arena start`
*   **Cost:** $100,000 (from the National Treasury).
*   **Duration:** 7 Days (Default).
*   **Limitations:** A maximum of 2 organized sessions per presidential term.
*   **Location:** Uses the area around World Spawn with a 100-block radius.

## 🎮 Gameplay

### Joining & Leaving
*   **Join:** `/dc arena join`
    *   Saves the player's current location.
    *   Clears the player's inventory (stored).
    *   Grants a standard **Arena Kit**.
    *   Teleports to the arena.
*   **Leave:** `/dc arena leave`
    *   Restores the player's original inventory.
    *   Teleports back to the original location.

### Arena Kit (Standard)
Every participant receives the same equipment to ensure fairness:
*   Full Iron Armor.
*   Iron Sword & Bow (32 Arrows).
*   3x Golden Apple & 16x Cooked Beef.

### Combat Rules
*   **Boundary:** Players leaving the arena radius will be automatically teleported back to the center.
*   **Respawn:** Players who die will respawn at a random location within the arena after 3 seconds, with brief *invulnerability* effects.
*   **Death Penalty:** Players who die are fined **$5,000** (taken from personal balance).

## 💰 Reward System

### 1. Kill Rewards (Per Kill)
Every time a player kills another, the killer receives an instant reward.
*   **Money:** +$5,000 (Added to Vault balance).
*   **Streak Bonus:** Additional rewards for reaching specific killstreaks.

### 2. Killstreak Bonus
Players who survive for a long time and kill many enemies in a row receive gear upgrades and buffs.

| Streak | Additional Reward | Buffs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5 Kills** | +$10,000 | 3x G-Apple, Speed I (30s) |
| **10 Kills** | +$25,000 | **Diamond Sword**, Strength I (30s) |
| **25 Kills** | +$50,000 | **Netherite Axe**, Strength II (60s) |
| **50 Kills** | +$100,000 | **Full Netherite Armor**, Strength/Resist (2m) |

### 3. Daily Leaderboard
Every day, the top 10 players with the most kills for that day receive rewards:
*   **1st Place:** $100,000 + 3 Netherite Ingots
*   **2nd Place:** $75,000 + 2 Netherite Ingots
*   **3rd Place:** $60,000 + 2 Netherite Ingots
*   *(Rewards decrease up to the 10th position)*

### 4. Grand Prize
Awarded to the player with the most total kills at the end of the event period (after 7 days).
*   **Cash Prize:** **$1,000,000**
*   **Trophy:** *Arena Champion Trophy* (Unique Nether Star).
*   **Item:** *Champion's Chestplate* (Special Netherite Chestplate).

## 📊 Data Management

Arena session data is stored in the `DataManager` (`arena_session.json` or within `data.yml`).
*   **Statistics:** Records Kills, Deaths, Current Streak, Best Streak, and Total Income per player.
*   **Leaderboard:** Stores daily ranking history.

## ⚙️ Configuration

Arena parameters can be modified in `config.yml`:

```yaml
arena:
  # Event start cost
  start-cost: 100000
  
  # Event duration (days)
  duration-days: 7
  
  # Arena radius from spawn (blocks)
  radius: 100
  
  # Base kill reward
  kill-reward: 5000
  
  # Death penalty
  death-penalty: 5000
  
  # End-of-event grand prize
  grand-prize: 1000000
```
