# Executive Orders System

This document provides a technical and comprehensive explanation of **Executive Orders**, the highest privilege held by the President to modify global server conditions through official decrees.

## 📋 Overview

Executive Orders are national-scale policies that have a significant impact on the *gameplay* of all players. Due to their immense power, their use is restricted by high costs from the **National Treasury** and long **Cooldown** periods.

## 🏛️ Terms of Use

1.  **Authority:** Only the currently serving **President** can issue these orders.
2.  **Cost:** Each order requires a large cost (Default: **$1,000,000**) taken from the National Treasury.
    *   If the treasury balance is insufficient, the order will fail to be issued.
3.  **Cooldown:** There is a delay between the use of executive orders (Default: **7 Days**).
4.  **Exclusivity:** The same order cannot be issued if it is still active.

## 📜 List of Executive Orders

The following is a complete list of available executive orders and their technical effects:

### 1. Golden Age Decree (`GOLDEN_AGE`)
*   **Duration:** 48 Hours
*   **Effect:** A golden era of prosperity.
    *   +25% XP Gain (Global)
    *   +25% Vault Income (Money)
    *   +15% Rare Item Drop Rate
*   **Usage:** Boosting the economy and general player progress.

### 2. State of Emergency (`STATE_OF_EMERGENCY`)
*   **Duration:** 24 Hours
*   **Effect:** Suspension of dangerous activities for safety.
    *   **PvP Disabled** worldwide (except in official Arenas).
*   **Usage:** Preventing chaos or uncontrolled wars between players.

### 3. National Holiday (`NATIONAL_HOLIDAY`)
*   **Duration:** 24 Hours
*   **Effect:** A national holiday for fun.
    *   Job cooldowns removed.
    *   Daily Rewards can be claimed twice (2x).
*   **Usage:** Increasing player engagement and providing a break from heavy grinding.

### 4. War Economy (`WAR_ECONOMY`)
*   **Duration:** 72 Hours
*   **Effect:** Economic mobilization for war.
    *   PvP Kill Rewards doubled.
    *   PvP Damage increased by +50%.
*   **Usage:** Encouraging PvP activity and faction conflict.

### 5. Economic Recovery Plan (`ECONOMIC_RECOVERY`)
*   **Duration:** 36 Hours
*   **Effect:** Massive economic stimulus.
    *   Direct **$50,000** subsidy to every online player (one-time claim).
    *   25% discount at all server shops.
*   **Usage:** Assisting new players and reviving a sluggish economy.

### 6. Infrastructure Initiative (`INFRASTRUCTURE_INITIATIVE`)
*   **Duration:** 72 Hours
*   **Effect:** Acceleration of national development.
    *   Provides **+100 free Claim Blocks**.
    *   Permanent **Haste II** effect for all players for the active duration.
*   **Usage:** Speeding up player base or city construction projects.

### 7. Environmental Protection Act (`ENVIRONMENTAL_PROTECTION`)
*   **Duration:** 48 Hours
*   **Effect:** Protection and enrichment of nature.
    *   **3x Drop Rate** for Farming, Fishing, and Breeding.
*   **Usage:** Benefiting players in farming or fishing professions.

### 8. Education Advancement (`EDUCATION_ADVANCEMENT`)
*   **Duration:** 48 Hours
*   **Effect:** Investment in knowledge.
    *   **3x XP Gain**.
    *   Enchanting costs cut by 50%.
    *   Free Anvil use (no XP cost).
*   **Usage:** Speeding up leveling and equipment upgrades.

### 9. Purge Protocol (`PURGE_PROTOCOL`)
*   **Duration:** 6 Hours
*   **Effect:** **⚠️ DANGEROUS!** Jungle law applies.
    *   PvP active EVERYWHERE (including safe zones).
    *   *Keep Inventory* disabled (items drop on death).
    *   Respawn locations randomized (Random Respawn).
*   **Usage:** Total chaos event ("The Purge"). Extremely high risk.

### 10. Presidential Pardon (`PRESIDENTIAL_PARDON`)
*   **Duration:** Instant (One-time Use)
*   **Effect:** Mass amnesty.
    *   Removes 1 active punishment (warn/mute) from the history of all online players.
*   **Usage:** Calming the atmosphere or providing a second chance for minor offenders.

## 🔧 Technical Mechanism

### Activation
*   **Command:** `/dc order <type>`
*   When activated:
    1.  Cost is deducted from the Treasury.
    2.  Cooldown period starts.
    3.  A global message is broadcast (includes Title screen & Dragon/Wither sound).
    4.  Visual lightning effect strikes at the President's location.

### Global Influence
The technical manager (`ExecutiveOrderManager`) attaches *hooks* to various event listeners to modify game parameters in *real-time*:
*   **XP & Drop:** Modifies `PlayerExpChangeEvent` and `BlockDropItemEvent`.
*   **PvP:** Cancels or modifies `EntityDamageByEntityEvent`.
*   **Economy:** Intervenes in Vault transactions and Shop plugins.

### Configuration
Basic parameters can be changed via `config.yml`:

```yaml
executive-orders:
  # Order issuance cost
  cost: 1000000
  
  # Cooldown between orders (days)
  cooldown-days: 7
```
