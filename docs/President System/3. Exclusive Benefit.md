# Presidential Perks

As the supreme leader, the President receives the strongest set of buffs on the server to ensure their safety and authority.

### 1. Status Effects (Permanent)
While in office, the President automatically receives permanent potion effects:
*   **Night Vision:** Infinite night vision.
*   **Saturation:** Never feel hungry (The hunger bar remains full).

### 2. Physical Attributes
Enhanced combat and self-defense capabilities:
*   **Attack Damage:** +15% Physical attack damage.
*   **Armor Toughness:** +1.2 Armor toughness points (Equivalent to an additional Diamond/Netherite set).
*   **Max Health:** +4 extra hearts (8 HP).

### 3. Economic & Progress Perks
*   **Daily Salary:** $5,000 (Automatically added to balance every day).
*   **Vault Bonus:** +20% more money from any source (Killing mobs/mining).
*   **XP Bonus:** +10% Experience gain.
